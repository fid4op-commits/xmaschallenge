📜 HOW TO PLAY & RULES ▼
  • Daily Scoring: Daily Chips = Base Chips × Active Multiplier.
  • Workouts: Gym / Home workouts grant 100 Base Chips. Workout Skip grants 25 Chips. Violations give 0 Chips. You have 5 Workout Skips total for the entire campaign (non-renewable).
  • Sugar Control: Sugar Free days add +1 Multiplier. Sugar Skip maintains your Mult. Sugar Violations reset Multiplier to 0.
  • Ante Goals & Rewards: Each Ante lasts 7 Rounds. Clear an Ante target to reach the next Ante and gain +1 Sugar Skip!
  • Perks: Select 1 active Perk per round for bonuses. Unselected Perks rotate automatically after each round.
